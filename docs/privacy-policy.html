Privacy Policy — Labors Near Me (लेबर्स नियर मी)

Effective date: 26 September 2026 · Contact: privacy@laborsnearme.in

Labors Near Me ("the App", "we", "us") helps users find local service providers (electricians, tailors, plumbers, masons, cooks and other workers) and lets workers list their services. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. This policy is for the Android/iOS app and the website.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your contacts list, SMS, call logs, or device files.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

3. How information is used

  • To provide the service: search, listings, contact (call/WhatsApp), reviews.
  • To verify and moderate listings (fraud/spam prevention).
  • To improve the app (aggregate, non-personal statistics like search counts).

4. Location

Location is used only to sort workers by distance. You can deny GPS permission and choose your city manually — all features keep working.

5. Sharing

  • Provider information you publish (name, business, phone, address) is public by design — that is the point of the service.
  • Your phone number is never shown publicly (only verified leads reach providers).
  • We may disclose information if required by Indian law or a valid court order.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS. Access to the database is restricted to authorized operators. No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

7. Data retention & deletion

We keep account data while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time by emailing privacy@laborsnearme.in from your registered email/phone. We will delete it within 30 days (except records we must keep by law).

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the app or on this page. Continued use means acceptance of the updated policy.
